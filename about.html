About OPH

OPH is a rigorous, consistent and complete description of the unified, self-creating computational Universe we inhabit.

Observer Patch Holography starts with finite observers on a closed screen. Each one keeps records and repairs its disagreements with its neighbours, using one local rule. What no repair can remove stays: protected defects, and they carry the classes of the Standard Model particles.

This site runs that universe in your browser and reads off its particles, from the first ticks to the lepton masses. Every step links to the derivation behind it.
