   characters 1, w, w^2 are the three generations of that class (lane L-NAT; Particles/04 face axis {4,5,11}/{6,7,8}).

   no three-way split of any class in 1,048 preregistered tests (Bonferroni, retested on level 9 and on the
   conjugate class); the only three-group property is lifetime, which separates fates within one run.
